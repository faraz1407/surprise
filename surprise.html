A Little Letter For The Birthday Girl

Meri Pyaari Sofiya

Begum sahiba, ye aapki life ka pehla birthday hai jisme main aur aap hum dono saath hain. Aur sach kahu babu, main is din ko aapke liye duniya ka sabse khaas din banana chahta hoon.

Meri pyaari Sofiya, aapke aane se meri zindagi sach mein mukammal ho gayi hai. Aaj se pehle chahe jitne bhi birthdays aapne manaye hon, par ab se aane wala har saal, har cake aur har khoobsurat lamha aur bhi special hoga.

Madam ji, aapki ek pyari si muskurahat ko dekhna ab meri sabse pyari aadat ban chuki hai. Aur meri dua hai ki ye muskurahat hamesha aise hi bani rahe.

Ye humara saath mein pehla birthday zaroor hai, meri wifey, par meri dua hai ki aane wale har birthday par hum isi tarah ek doosre ka saath celebrate karein. Aap meri zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat hissa ho aur hamesha rahogi.

Happy Birthday meri jaan ❤️ Aaj ka din sirf aapka hai.

With all my love, always ♥
